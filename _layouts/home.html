Vyacheslav (Slava) Shen

Machine Learning Engineer

Hi, I'm Slava. I actually got my start studying Aerospace and Electrical Engineering at KAIST (where I earned my B.S. and M.S.), but I eventually found my true focus in computer vision. Today, I'm a Senior Machine Learning Engineer working on AI that solves incredibly complex, real-world problems. I've spent a lot of my career deep in medical imaging, localizing brain stroke in CT scans and detecting Alzheimer's disease from MRIs, and working on fascinating multimodal challenges like decoding fMRI data into text (literally reading people's minds). Currently, I'm at Verigram working on biometric security, specifically, making anti-spoofing (PAD) systems smarter and much harder to trick.
